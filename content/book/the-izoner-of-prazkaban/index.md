Happy Rotter, torn away from his best friends, Nor and Herminey, is about to start his first year at Prazkaban, a foreboding school to the South. Happy can’t believe how well he's treated. (Who wouldn’t if they lived at Warthogs?)

But when Happy gets to Prazkaban, the atmosphere is relaxed. There’s a mentally unbalanced headmaster who happens to be Codra's father, and the sinister Serious White has been called in to be Happy’s designated friend...
