# Summer 1991

I’m recording this in the hopes that my life will become interesting.

So says young Lydia Pasternack, who is moving with her family to a small town in Oregon. 

This summer will, indeed, change her life forever. 

She just doesn’t know it yet.

For the town holds a secret.

The secret is... not a good one. 

Her parents are in on it. 

Every grown-up is in on it.

When a horrific murder takes place, Lydia must take agency and solve the mystery that this town is hiding... for better or for worse...
